FKP_3 v1.37

Mouse click reliability update:
- Enables retrieval of interactive accessibility windows.
- Left click first tries ACTION_CLICK on the accessibility node under the cursor.
- Falls back to AccessibilityService.dispatchGesture for arbitrary screen content.
- Right click attempts ACTION_CONTEXT_CLICK where the target exposes it; Android does not provide a universal mouse-button injection API through AccessibilityService.
- No keyboard layout changes.
