FKP_3 v1.45 - Adjustable Mouse Window Transparency

Changes:
- Added a transparency control (SeekBar) to the mouse window.
- Transparency can be adjusted from 20% to 100% opacity.
- The selected value is shown as a percentage.
- The selected transparency is saved and restored the next time the mouse window is opened.
- The entire mouse panel, including its controls, follows the selected opacity.
- Existing mouse dragging, resizing, close button, clickable-target snap, and left/right click controls are preserved.
- No keyboard layout changes were made.

Note: Android Build was not executed in this environment; ZIP integrity was verified.
