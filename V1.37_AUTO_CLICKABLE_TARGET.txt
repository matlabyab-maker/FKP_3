FKP_3 v1.37
================
Added a toggle button to the mouse overlay: "حرکت خودکار".
When enabled, the Accessibility service periodically scans the active screen's
Accessibility tree and moves the cursor to visible clickable controls (including
accessible links and buttons). It does NOT click them automatically.
Press the button again to disable automatic targeting.
The mode stops automatically when the mouse overlay is hidden or the service is destroyed.

This feature depends on the Android Accessibility service being enabled and the
target application exposing clickable controls through its Accessibility tree.
