FKP_3 v1.34 - Quick Settings Mouse

A Quick Settings tile named "موس FKP_3" was added.
When the Accessibility mouse service is enabled, tapping the tile toggles only the mouse overlay:
- desktop-style navy cursor
- touch field that moves the cursor over the whole screen
- left/right click controls

The overlay is independent of the keyboard, so it can be used while browsing websites or using other apps.
Turning the tile off removes both the mouse field and cursor.

Add the tile from Android Quick Settings edit screen after installing the APK.
